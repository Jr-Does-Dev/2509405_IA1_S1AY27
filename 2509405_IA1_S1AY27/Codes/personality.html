Critical Essay

A Tool, Not a Crutch: Using LLMs as a Student

A tool helps me do something better, while a crutch does the work I should be doing myself.
If an LLM writes my code or essay, I get a grade but not the skill. Bastani et al. (2025)
found that students who freely used GPT-4 for maths practice did worse on later exams without it,
while students using a hint-based tutor version avoided that drop. How I use it matters most.
            
As a student, I can use LLMs to explain difficult concepts in simpler ways (Kasneci et al., 2023),
to help me understand why my code fails, to quiz me before a test, and to give feedback on drafts
I have written myself. I should not use them to complete graded work or accept their answers
without checking, since they can sound confident and still be wrong (UNESCO, 2023).
A good test is whether I can explain the answer without the tool.
            
Used to get unstuck and to test my understanding, LLMs improve my learning. Used to skip the
thinking, they weaken the skills I am here to build.
